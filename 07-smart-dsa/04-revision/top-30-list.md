## Week-8 Sprint Instructions

The cold-solve sprint runs across Days 53, 54, and 56. Do all three batches in order. Do not skip ahead or preview problems before your batch starts.

### Batch 1 — Day 53 (Problems 1–10)

Problems 1–10 from the table above (Two Sum through Koko Eating Bananas).

- 30 minutes per problem, strict timer.
- Open a blank file. No notes, no pattern guides, no previous stubs.
- After each problem: score yourself using the sheet below, then move on immediately — no fixups mid-batch.

### Batch 2 — Day 54 (Problems 11–20)

Problems 11–20 (Min Stack through Number of Islands).

Same rules as Batch 1.

### Batch 3 — Day 56 (Problems 21–30)

Problems 21–30 (Course Schedule through Word Ladder).

Same rules. After Batch 3: run gap analysis — list every problem scored < 4. Day 57 is for targeted re-solves of those problems only.
