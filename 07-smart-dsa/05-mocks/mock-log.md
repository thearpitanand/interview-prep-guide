## Week-over-Week Trend

After each mock, read this section and update the notes below the table.

### How to Use This Section

1. **After Mock #2:** Compare Mock #2 total vs Mock #1. Note any axis that dropped by 1 or more.
2. **After Mock #3 and #4:** Because #3 and #4 are back-to-back, compare #4 to #3 — a drop is normal (fatigue). Compare both to #2 for the real trend signal.
3. **After Mock #5:** This is the mid-course check. If any axis has not improved since Mock #1, it is a structural gap — add a deliberate drill in the daily problems for that axis.
4. **After Mock #7:** Compare Mock #7 to Mock #1. Total should have increased. Any axis still at 1 or 2 requires a human mock before your real interview loop.

### Escalation Rules

- **Total < 15/25 by Mock #4:** Stop self-mocks. Schedule a human mock with a peer or on Pramp/Interviewing.io before continuing. Self-mocks are not giving you enough adversarial pressure.
- **Any single axis at 1 across three consecutive mocks:** That axis is not self-correcting. Re-read `01-method/interview-framework.md` and do a 20-minute focused drill (practice that behavior on a problem you have already solved, no new content).
- **Total ≥ 20/25 on Mock #5 or later:** You are in strong-hire territory. Use remaining mocks to push the weak axis to 5 rather than improve the total.
